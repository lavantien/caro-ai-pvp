## The alloc-t gap is the solver law

The solver wiring (internal/server/match.go, solverSearcher) runs the VCF pass then the VCT pass, each under its own fixed budget of SolverBudgetShare 0.5 times the move grant capped at SolverNodeBudget 2^20 nodes. A proof ends the move with the [VCF] or [VCT] tag. A miss hands the unspent remainder to the inner standard search. The M-line bills the whole grant as alloc while t counts only the inner search, so alloc minus t measures the solver passes' share of the grant:

- Hard at 3+2: 5.82 alloc against 4.04 t, 1.78 s or about 30% of the mean grant spent before the standard search (VCF plus VCT).
- Medium at 3+2 (VCF only): 5.23 against 4.72, 0.51 s or about 10%.
- Hard at 1+0: 1.27 against 0.97, about 24%, and in the worst case both passes consume their halves fully, leaving the inner search a near-empty budget. That is the mechanism behind the 1+0 depth collapse.
- 541 hard lines at 1+0 carry d=0, n=0, s=+0, t=0.00 with alloc=0.01: drained endgame grants where the search completes no iteration and the move lands on the documented best-ordered-candidate fallback.

## d=64 is the ply cap in forced endgames

Max depth 64 appears on every tier at 3+2 (only easy at 1+0). Spot checks in the marathon games (stones past move 240 of 256) show d=64 lines with score +0, ebf 2.0, and PVs that walk the remaining empty cells in order. This is SearchMaxPly reached by iterative deepening on a near-full board where every line runs to the leaves, not a parse artifact of the logstats analyzer.

## Verdict

The tiers run exactly the spec resources: easy 1 core, no table, no solvers; medium 2 cores, 32 MiB table, VCF; hard 4 cores, 128 MiB table, VCF plus VCT. Nothing handicaps easy or medium, and no color compensation exists anywhere. Under those specs the strength order the evidence gives is:

- easy is the weakest tier at both controls, by a wide margin.
- medium over hard is real and reproduces at both controls: the full band at 1+0, medium-1 over hard-1 at 3+2.
- the mechanism the telemetry isolates is the solver share: hard's VCF plus VCT passes take 24 to 30% of every move grant before the standard search runs, which at 1+0 collapses its depth below medium's and at 3+2 costs it conversion despite dominating every raw search metric.

The spec'd difficulty ladder (easy < medium < hard) does not hold as written at these controls. Per the standing law the tiers are not retuned here: this document records what the specs produce, and any change to the solver share, budget law, or tier wiring is a spec decision that starts a new evidence chain.
