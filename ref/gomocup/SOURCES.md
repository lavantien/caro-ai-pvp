Archive contents: HUNGAR01 report from the first Hungarian computer gomoku tournament; BNAIC paper on probabilistic opponent-model search; DF-PN depth-first proof-number search; GA_SENET genetic algorithms (Czech); GO-MOKU-AND-THREAT gomoku and threat-space search; PROOF-SET-SEARCH; SCHIJF93PROOFNUMBER proof-number search and transpositions; ALGORITMY 5 search-related articles.

The two protocol pages are linked from detail-information (the developers page itself links only templates, engines, and the paper archives above); they are the gomocup brain-to-manager API specification, protocol 1 over text files and protocol 2 over stdin/stdout.
