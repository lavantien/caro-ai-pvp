## root cause verdict 2026-10-10

Every inflated row shares one mechanism. The t column measures the ponder lane round trip from the moment the opponent's move lands, and the lane is one serial goroutine serving both seats. After each move the mover's arm enqueues a start, and solverSearcher.StartPonder runs its VCF then VCT passes on the ponder board under a nil deadline before dispatching the SMP ponder job. On quiet boards the VCT pass neither proves nor prunes under the 1<<20 node budget and burns it whole: 1048577 nodes at measured 30-70k nps single-threaded is 15-50 s of wall time. The next stop queues behind that burn, the stopped seat's turn absorbs the remainder, the adopted [PONDER] M-line carries it as t, and applyMoveLocked commits the inflated elapsed to that seat's clock. The stall is seat-symmetric, appears only with ponder enabled, clusters at quiet early boards like M7, and shows at every increment because the pass had no clock bound at all.

Direct measurement on the exact probe boards via playground/solverpass: the 7-stone game 3 ponder board burns the full 1048577 VCT nodes in 33.1 s, the 33-stone game 1 ponder board in 42.2 s, VCF exits in 1-3 ms. Fix: the ponder solver passes moved off the lane. solverSearcher.StartPonder dispatches the inward SMP ponder immediately and runs its VCF/VCT passes for the ponder board in a side goroutine under a pure stop flag, so the pass runs from the mover's move until the ponder owner's next turn, however long, and StopPonder trips and joins it before collecting. No wall-clock constant exists anywhere on the path, the only bounds are the opponent's actual turn and the solver's own node budget, the lane never stalls, and the stalled-clock damage class is gone.
